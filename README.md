### 2. AI-Powered Data Analysis — India's Digital Education Readiness

**Objective:**  
Analyze India's digital education readiness using school infrastructure data and rural learner-level evidence.

**Data Sources:**
- UDISE+ 2023–24
- UDISE+ 2024–25
- ASER 2024
- Government education resources

**Analysis Areas:**
- School internet availability
- Computer availability
- Smartphone access and usage
- Educational versus social-media smartphone usage
- Gender gap in smartphone ownership
- Year-over-year infrastructure changes

**Selected Findings:**
- Schools with internet facilities increased from 53.9% in 2023–24 to 63.5% in 2024–25.
- Schools with computers reached 64.7% in 2024–25.
- 57% of rural 14–16-year-olds who knew how to use a smartphone reported using it for educational activity in the previous week.
- Smartphone ownership was reported at 36.2% for boys and 26.9% for girls.

**Analytical Focus:**  
The analysis examined the difference between digital access, digital capability, and purposeful educational use.

**AI-Assisted Work:**
- Research support
- Source discovery
- Summarization
- Percentage-point calculations
- Comparative analysis
- Report structuring and visualization support

Statistics were checked against the cited original sources.

[View Report](reports/task-02-digital-education-analysis/India_Digital_Education_Readiness_Analysis.pdf)

### 3. Volunteer, Internship & Fellowship Opportunity Data Compilation

**Objective:**  
Create a structured resource of volunteer opportunities, internships, and fellowships available to students in India.

**Scope:**
- 18 opportunities compiled
- 9 volunteering opportunities
- 5 internship opportunities
- 4 fellowship opportunities

**Data Fields:**
- Organization
- Opportunity type
- Eligibility
- Application link
- Deadline / application cycle
- Role or program description

**Research Approach:**

**Key Outcome:**

### 4. AI Insights for NGO Growth

**Objective:**  
Identify practical AI-driven strategies that can strengthen NGO outreach, volunteer engagement, fundraising, and online presence.

**Five Areas Explored:**

1. Predictive Donor & Retention Analytics
2. AI Chatbots for Outreach and Supporter Queries
3. AI-Assisted Content Creation for Social Media and Campaigns
4. Volunteer Matching and Scheduling Automation
5. AI-Powered Analytics Dashboards for Impact Reporting

**Strategic Focus:**
- Donor retention
- Supporter communication
- Content operations
- Volunteer coordination
- Impact reporting
- Data-driven decision making

**Implementation Approach:**  
The report recommends piloting one AI capability against a specific organizational bottleneck, measuring the result over a defined period, and scaling only after evaluating the outcome.

Human review was retained as an important control for AI-assisted processes, particularly where donor or beneficiary data is involved.

Analyze the Foundation's programs, publicly reported impact indicators, digital presence, organizational challenges, and opportunities for improvement.

**Analysis Areas:**

- Publicly reported impact metrics

- Udaan
